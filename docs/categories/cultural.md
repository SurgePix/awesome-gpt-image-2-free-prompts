## East Asia

### Tokyo Street Scene
```
Shibuya crossing, Tokyo, golden hour. Hundreds of pedestrians mid-crossing, 
motion blur, umbrellas. Giant LED billboards with Japanese text. 
Shot from elevated position, 50mm equivalent. Cinematic grade: warm highlights, 
teal shadows. NHK documentary aesthetic.
```

### Traditional Courtyard
```
Chinese traditional courtyard (siheyuan), spring morning. 
Red lanterns, plum blossom tree in bloom, elderly man doing tai chi. 
Architecture: grey brick, curved roof tiles. Soft morning mist. 
Ink-wash painting influence meeting photorealism. 
Warm and peaceful atmosphere.
```

### Seoul Night Market
```
Seoul Gwangjang market at night, close-up on a bindaetteok (mung bean pancake) 
vendor stall. Ajumma vendor in apron, skillfully flipping pancakes. 
Steam, golden sizzle, orange tungsten light. Tourists and locals crowded around. 
Street food documentary photography, 35mm film aesthetic.
```

## South & Southeast Asia

### Indian Festival
```
Holi festival celebration, India. Bird's eye view of crowd throwing 
colored powder — vivid magenta, yellow, blue clouds. Joyful faces, 
white kurtas stained with color. Golden afternoon light cuts through 
colored haze. Documentary photography, wide angle 24mm, 
high-energy composition.
```

### Thai Temple
```
Wat Phra Kaew, Bangkok, early morning before crowds. 
Grand Palace complex, gilded spires catching first light. 
Buddhist monks in saffron robes walking single file on stone path. 
Mist rising from surrounding gardens. Architectural photography, 
symmetrical composition, 70mm telephoto.
```

## Middle East & Africa

### Moroccan Medina
```
Medina alley in Marrakech, Morocco. Narrow passage between 
ochre-colored walls, carved wooden doors, hanging lanterns. 
Local man on moped passing through, motion blur. 
Shafts of sunlight from gaps above. Travel photography, 
rich warm palette, authentic documentary feel.
```

### Latin American Street Art
```
Mexican street mural in progress. Large-scale artwork on concrete wall: 
jaguar with traditional Huichol geometric patterns, vivid colors. 
Artist on scaffolding, brush in hand, visible at 1/4 scale. 
Afternoon sun, saturated colors. Documentary photography, 
wide angle showing full mural and urban context.
```
