## Editorial Illustration

### Op-Ed Concept
```
Editorial illustration for a newspaper op-ed about AI and employment. 
Flat vector style, limited palette (navy, coral, cream). 
A robot hand and human hand reaching toward a shared desk. 
Conceptual, not literal. New Yorker / NYT illustration aesthetic.
```

### Infographic Illustration
```
Isometric illustration of a smart city. Buildings with solar panels, 
electric buses, cyclists, pedestrians. Data visualization overlays: 
Wi-Fi signals, energy flow lines. Clean flat design, vibrant colors. 
Information design meets editorial illustration.
```

## Children's Book

### Forest Adventure
```
Watercolor children's book illustration. A small fox wearing a red scarf 
walks through a misty autumn forest. Mushrooms and fallen leaves at ground level. 
Warm, safe, magical atmosphere. Loose brushwork, visible paper texture. 
Full page illustration, no text.

## Concept Art

### Sci-Fi Environment
```
Concept art for a sci-fi game environment. Abandoned space station exterior, 
asteroid field background, distant gas giant. Industrial design with visible 
decay: rust, torn solar panels. Painterly style, dramatic lighting. 
ArtStation portfolio quality, 16:9 horizontal composition.
```

### Fantasy Character
```
Character concept art, full body. A desert nomad mage, female, 40s, 
flowing sand-colored robes with blue geometric patterns. Staff with crystal top. 
Expression: focused, weathered. Three views: front, 3/4, back silhouette. 
Clean white background. Blizzard Entertainment character design aesthetic.
