## Portraits

### Barista Portrait
```
Ultra-realistic close-up portrait of a 28-year-old barista, warm smile, 
apron dusted with coffee grounds. Shot in a cafe at 7 AM, soft window light 
from the left. Sony A7IV, 85mm f/1.4, shallow depth of field. 
Warm, intimate atmosphere. Skin texture visible, slight film grain.
```

### Street Vendor
```
Photorealistic portrait of an elderly street food vendor, Southeast Asian, 
70s, weathered hands preparing noodles over a flame. Night market setting, 
orange tungsten light from above, steam rising. Leica Q2, 28mm, 
environmental portrait style, documentary photography.
```

### Corporate Headshot
```
Professional corporate headshot, 35-year-old woman, business casual blazer, 
confident but approachable expression. Studio lighting: large softbox from 
upper left, reflector fill on right. Clean grey background. Canon R5, 
135mm f/2, commercial photography standard.
```

## Street & Urban

### Rainy Night Street
```
Ultra-realistic urban street scene at 11 PM, heavy rain. Wet cobblestones 
reflecting neon signs in red and blue. One person walking with umbrella, 
silhouette only, mid-distance. Shot handheld, Sony 24mm f/1.8, motion blur 
in raindrops, 1/30s shutter. Hong Kong alley aesthetic.
```

### Morning Commute
```
Tokyo subway platform at 8:30 AM rush hour. Hundreds of commuters, 
motion blurred, suits and umbrellas. One woman standing still in focus, 
reading phone, white mask, blue coat. Canon 16-35mm f/2.8, wide angle, 
fluorescent overhead light. Street photography style, candid.
```

### Convenience Store Night
```
Ultra-realistic urban street group photo at a convenience store entrance 
at 10 PM on a summer night. 3-4 young people briefly chatting at the entrance, 
casual clothing, neon signs reflected on wet pavement. Sony A7IV, 35mm f/1.4, 
shallow depth of field, natural ambient light mixed with store fluorescence.
```

## Nature & Landscape

### Golden Hour Field
```
Photorealistic golden hour landscape, wheat field in late summer, 
Central Europe. Sun 15 degrees above horizon, backlit, lens flare 
in upper right corner. A lone oak tree at the rule-of-thirds intersection. 
Shot on Nikon Z9, 24-70mm at 35mm, f/8, polarizer filter. 
Rich warm tones, cinematic grade.
```

### Foggy Mountain
```
Ultra-realistic misty mountain valley at dawn, layers of fog between 
pine-covered ridges. No people. Telephoto compression, apparent from 
200mm lens, f/11, tripod shot. Cool blue-grey palette with subtle 
warm glow where sun breaks through. Landscape photography, fine art print quality.
```

## Interior & Architecture

### Cafe Interior
```
Photorealistic interior of a specialty coffee shop, daytime. 
Industrial-Scandinavian fusion: exposed concrete, warm wood, 
pendant Edison bulbs. 3 patrons visible, blurred, depth of field. 
Shot with wide-angle 16mm, f/5.6, natural window light dominant. 
Architectural photography style.
```

### Brutalist Building
```
Exterior of a 1970s brutalist apartment block, overcast sky, 
wet after rain. Extreme symmetry, shot from ground level looking up, 
16mm ultra-wide. No people. Grey concrete texture hyper-detailed. 
Black and white with slight blue tint, fine art architectural photography.
```

### Coffee Flat Lay
```
Overhead flat lay, specialty coffee on cream linen, rustic ceramic cup, 
small milk pitcher, two coffee beans as props. Natural north-facing window light. 
Shot directly overhead, 50mm macro, f/8. Warm neutral tones, 
food styling, commercial photography. No text.
```

### Market Produce

```
Ultra-realistic close-up of heirloom tomatoes at a farmers market stall, 
various colors — deep red, yellow, green striped. Water droplets on skin.
```
